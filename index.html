How to play

Each player starts with one secret card. On your turn, draw one card, choose which of your two cards to keep, then play the other and resolve its effect. Played cards stay visible. If you are eliminated, you sit out until the next round.

When the draw pile is empty, the highest card in hand wins the round. If tied, compare the highest card each tied player has played. First to 3 tokens wins.

Two-player setup: each player gets one card; three cards are removed from the deck (two shown face-up, one hidden).
